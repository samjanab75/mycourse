About Tea Farming

Tea farming is one of the most important agricultural activities in India. Tea plants need cool climate, rainfall and fertile soil.

India is famous for producing Assam Tea, Nilgiri Tea and Darjeeling Tea.

Types of Tea

  1. Green Tea
  2. Black Tea
  3. Herbal Tea
  4. Masala Tea

Benefits of Tea

  • Improves freshness
  • Boosts energy
  • Good for health
  • Rich in antioxidants
