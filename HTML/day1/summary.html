INSPIRING SHORT STORY OF A GIRL

There was a young girl who was very quiet and always afraid to speak in front of others. Whenever she saw confident people, she thought, “I can never be like them.” One day, her teacher asked everyone to speak for one minute in class. She was nervous, her hands were shaking, and she made a few mistakes. Some students laughed softly, and she felt embarrassed. But instead of giving up, she decided to practice every day. She stood in front of a mirror, improved her speaking little by little, and started believing in herself. At first, even 30 seconds felt difficult. After a few months, she could speak confidently in front of many people. Years later, she became the person others admired for confidence.

Moral of story
Confidence is not something people are born with.It grows when you keep trying, even when you feel afraid.
Small steps every day can change your life.

The Hands That Once Held Mine

A small boy used to visit his grandmother every evening after school. She would wait for him at the door with a warm smile and his favorite snacks. One day, the boy noticed that his grandmother looked weak and tired, but she still smiled and asked about his day. As time passed, she became very sick and could no longer walk properly. The boy started helping her by bringing water, reading stories to her, and sitting beside her quietly. He realized that the same hands that once cared for him now needed his care. One evening, the grandmother held his hand and said, “The love you give to others always comes back to you.” After she passed away, the house felt empty, but the boy never forgot her words. He grew up understanding that true love is shown not only in happy moments but also in care, patience, and being there for someone when they need it most.

Moral of the Story

True love is shown through care, kindness, and being there for the people who once cared for us.
